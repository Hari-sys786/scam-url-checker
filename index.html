How ScamCheck works

  1. Local heuristics — checks domain age signals, suspicious TLDs, IP-based URLs, excessive subdomains, and known scam keyword patterns
  2. Google Safe Browsing — queries Google's threat database for known phishing, malware, and social engineering URLs
  3. VirusTotal — cross-references 70+ security vendors for additional threat intelligence

All three signals are combined into a single verdict. If external APIs are unavailable, heuristics alone are used with a notice.
